    Choosing the right automation tool depends on your project's needs, technology stack, and scalability requirements. WebDriverIO + Appium is great for full-stack JavaScript users, TestNG + Selenium + Appium suits Java-based teams, and Playwright + Appium is ideal for modern web automation. Evaluate based on ecosystem support, ease of setup, and cross-platform capabilities to optimize your automation strategy.
