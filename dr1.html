Heritage & Culinary Passion

Crafting Soulful Culinary Memories Since 2018

At Savor & Soul, food is more than nourishment—it is a narrative told through temperature, texture, and rare seasonal ingredients. Executive Chef Antoine Vane brings two decades of Michelin-starred culinary wisdom to create harmonious pairings.

Every ingredient is meticulously sourced from local organic farms, high-altitude vineyards, and sustainable artisanal fisheries to minimize footprint while elevating flavor.
