A kitchen, not a factory.

We started in a home kitchen in 2019 with one oven and a recipe for saffron milk cake. Today we bake in small batches every morning, using Amul butter, local milk and Gujarat-grown pistachios and cardamom.

If a bake is sold out, it is sold out. We would rather run out than sell yesterday's.
