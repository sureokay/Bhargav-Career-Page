6. Year-by-year

How it is calculated, and its limits

  • Each month: corpus grows at the net monthly rate, then the withdrawal is taken. Net return = index return − expense ratio.
  • Tax: for each withdrawal, the share that is a gain (current value minus amount invested) is tracked. At year end, tax = rate × (gains that year − exemption), paid out of the corpus. This is a simplified average-cost model, not a tax filing.
  • Real, volatile markets do not return a steady figure. A bad early patch can drain the corpus even if the long-run average is fine, so use the stress test.
  • Not included: exit loads, STT, surcharge/cess, other income, changes in tax law, or your actual fund's tracking error.
